Limits & FAQ

Read this before you put real money behind an agent.

Honest limits

What Griffin can't do

  • Griffin is a server key. If it goes down, agent moves stop, your funds don't: the owner dismisses Griffin and signs alone.
  • The hard wall is the limits. Inside them, a bad route through an allowed program could still lose value. Keep limits to what you'd accept losing in a day.
  • Each agent move opens a proposal that holds about 0.004 SOL of rent from the agent key. A passed move closes it and the rent goes back to the vault.
  • Very long Jupiter routes may not fit one Solana transaction through Squads.
  • Griffin has not been audited. Start small.
FAQ

Questions

Can Griffin take my funds?

No. Griffin's key is a vote-only member. It can't propose or execute a move, and one vote is never enough. The worst a broken Griffin can do is vote yes on a move your agent proposed, inside a charter you signed.

What if the agent key leaks?

Whoever has it gets the agent's powers and no more: proposals that Griffin judges against your charter. Swap the agent key from the app in one transaction.

What if Griffin disappears?

Dismiss it from the app. You are the config authority, so it takes one transaction and no vote. The wallet then answers to your key alone.

Why Squads?

Squads v4 is the multisig most Solana teams already trust with treasury funds. Your funds sit in a Squads vault, not in anything we wrote.

Does it cost anything?

Guarding a wallet is free. You pay Solana fees and the proposal rent, which comes back to the vault when a move passes.

Is the code open?

Yes: the policy engine, the worker, the agent kit and the site. GitHub.
