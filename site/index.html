The policy

What Griffin checks

You set the numbers and sign them with the owner key. Griffin reads the move from chain, runs it in a simulation, then holds it to these articles.

  1. SOL per moveThe most one move may take out, counted from the simulation, wrapped SOL included.
  2. SOL per dayA rolling 24 hours of everything Griffin let through.
  3. RecipientsPlain SOL or token transfers only reach wallets on your list.
  4. ProgramsSystem, token, ATA, memo and Jupiter by default. Anything else you add by address.
  5. No spendersApprovals, authority changes and closing accounts into another wallet are always turned back.
  6. Something backA swap that pays out and returns nothing to the wallet is turned back.
  7. Token limitsPer move and per day caps for any token you name, a buy list if you want one.
  8. Your movesMoves the owner key proposes pass without a check. It's your wallet.
For agents

Give your bot the wallet, not the vault

  1. Open the app, connect your wallet, paste the agent's public key, create.
  2. Sign a policy. Until you do, every agent move is turned back.
  3. Fund the vault, and give the agent key a little SOL for fees and rent.
  4. Run the Griffin MCP server next to your agent. It proposes, asks Griffin and executes.

Tools: griffin_status, griffin_send_sol, griffin_send_token, griffin_swap. Hosted read-only MCP at /mcp: griffin_check, griffin_policy.

Honest limits

  • Griffin is a server key. If it goes down, agent moves stop, your funds don't: the owner dismisses Griffin and signs alone.
  • The hard wall is the SOL limits. Inside them, a clever route through an allowed program could still lose value. Keep limits to what you'd accept losing in a day.
  • Each agent move opens a proposal that costs about 0.004 SOL in rent from the agent key. A passed move closes it and the rent goes back to the vault.
  • Large Jupiter routes may not fit one Solana transaction through Squads. The agent kit asks for compact routes.
  • Griffin has not been audited. Start small.
