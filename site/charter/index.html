II

The charter

You set the numbers and seal them with the owner key. Griffin holds every agent move to these articles.

  1. SOL per moveThe most one move may take out, counted from the simulation, wrapped SOL included.
  2. SOL per dayA rolling 24 hours of everything Griffin let through.
  3. RecipientsPlain SOL or token transfers only reach wallets on your list. SOL wrapped into the wallet's own account stays the wallet's.
  4. ProgramsSystem, Token, Token-2022, ATA, Compute Budget, Memo and Jupiter v6 by default. Anything else you add by address.
  5. No spendersApprovals, authority changes and closing accounts into another wallet are always turned back.
  6. Something backA move that pays out and returns nothing to the wallet is turned back.
  7. Token limitsPer move and per day caps for any token you name, and a buy list if you want one.
  8. Your movesMoves the owner key proposes pass without a check. It's your wallet.
The seal

Only the owner signs it

The charter is a message your wallet signs. It is free and moves nothing. Griffin checks the signature against the wallet's config authority, so an agent can't loosen its own leash, and an old charter can't be replayed over a newer one.

GRIFFIN policy
wallet: <your Squads wallet>
hash: <sha256 of the charter>
nonce: <time you signed>
Whatever the charter says

Always turned back

  • Letting anyone else spend the wallet's tokens (approve).
  • Changing who owns or can close a token account.
  • System calls other than a plain transfer.
  • A program that is not on the list, anywhere at the top of the move.
  • A simulated result where a delegate, an owner or a close authority changes.
  • A move that fails in simulation, and any wallet with no charter yet.
